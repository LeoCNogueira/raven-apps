What people ask before downloading

Do I need all three apps?

No. Each app works on its own and is bought separately. Start with the one that solves today’s problem.

Is it a subscription?

No. The download is free and the unlock is a one-time purchase on the Mac App Store, valid on every Mac signed in with the same Apple Account. Prices are shown in your local currency on the Mac App Store.

Does the free trial charge anything?

No. The 14-day trials don’t renew and are never charged. When they end, your projects and files stay exactly as they are.

Can the apps delete or damage my files?

No. FlightFrame only reads the card, Acervo never overwrites or deletes and can undo any reorganization, and Guardian only reads the folders you choose.

Will it run on my Mac?

All three run on Mac computers with Apple silicon or Intel. Acervo requires macOS 13 or later, Guardian macOS 14, and FlightFrame macOS 15.

Does it work with a NAS and Lightroom?

Yes. All three work with external drives and NAS volumes mounted on your Mac, such as Synology and QNAP. FlightFrame integrates directly with Lightroom Classic, and Acervo keeps XMP sidecars with their photos.

Does the FAA check replace my authorization?

No. FlightFrame compares each flight with the authorization you already have, for information only. You still get authorization through LAANC or the FAA, and the remote pilot in command remains responsible for every flight.

Is there a Windows version?

No. The apps are built for Mac.
